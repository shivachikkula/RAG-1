# Nimbus Robotics - Company Overview

Nimbus Robotics is a fictional warehouse-automation company founded in 2019 by Priya Raman and Tomas Lindqvist in Gothenburg, Sweden. Priya Raman serves as Chief Executive Officer and Tomas Lindqvist serves as Chief Technology Officer.

The company builds autonomous mobile robots for logistics centers. Its headquarters moved to Copenhagen, Denmark in 2023, while the original Gothenburg office became the main research and development lab.

Nimbus Robotics raised a Series B round of 48 million euros in March 2024, led by Northwind Ventures. The funding is being used to expand manufacturing and to open a sales office in Rotterdam.
