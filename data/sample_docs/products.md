# Nimbus Robotics - Product Line

The Stratus S1 is the flagship autonomous mobile robot. It carries payloads up to 600 kilograms, runs for 10 hours on a single charge, and recharges to 80 percent in 45 minutes. The Stratus S1 navigates with lidar and uses the Cirrus fleet software for coordination.

The Cumulus C2 is a compact picking robot designed for narrow aisles. It has a payload limit of 120 kilograms and a battery life of 14 hours. The Cumulus C2 was launched in September 2024.

Cirrus is the fleet management software that coordinates all Nimbus robots. Cirrus assigns tasks, plans collision-free routes, and schedules charging. Cirrus was designed by the team led by Tomas Lindqvist and is licensed per robot per month.
