# Nimbus Robotics - Warranty and Support Policy

Every Stratus S1 and Cumulus C2 robot ships with a 24-month limited warranty covering parts and labor. Batteries are covered for 18 months or 3,000 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

Customers on the Premium Support plan receive a 4-hour on-site response time and a dedicated technical account manager. Customers on the Standard Support plan receive next-business-day response.

To file a warranty claim, customers open a ticket in the Cirrus dashboard and attach the robot's diagnostic log. Damage caused by operating a robot above its rated payload is not covered by the warranty.
