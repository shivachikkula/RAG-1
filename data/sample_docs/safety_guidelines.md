# Nimbus Robotics - Safety Guidelines

All Nimbus robots comply with the ISO 3691-4 standard for driverless industrial trucks. Each robot has two emergency stop buttons and a safety-rated lidar field that slows the robot when a person comes within 2 meters and stops it within 0.5 meters.

Operators must complete the Nimbus Safety Certification course before working alongside robots. The course takes 3 hours and certification must be renewed every 12 months.

If a robot behaves unexpectedly, operators should press the nearest emergency stop, then report the incident through the Cirrus dashboard within 24 hours.
